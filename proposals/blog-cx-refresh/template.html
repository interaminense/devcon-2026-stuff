Surf Journal CX Refresh

The blog page runs four client extensions on top of the post list. Each one works, but they were built one at a time: thick outlines that read as errors, a toggle and a widget floating over the cards, and a dashed striped box that looks unfinished. This proposal gives them one visual language, the Swell Lab one, and keeps the DEVCON colors for the moments where AI acts.

Post performanceBadges and thick outlines become a quiet footer on each card: share of clicks, a meter with the average marked, a status chip.
Advisor barThe floating "Content advisor: on" pill moves above the grid as a slim bar with the period, the legend and an on/off switch.
AI suggestionThe tall modal becomes a side panel that compares the current title and subtitle with the suggested ones.
What to write nextThe dashed, striped box becomes a clean section: posts ranked by click rate with bars, AI ideas beside them.
Live page viewsThe open widget that covers the cards becomes a collapsed pill with a sparkline; the full chart opens on click.

Post performance on the cards

client-extension-content-advisor/ldp-content-advisor.js
  • The share of clicks moves from a colored badge above the tag to a footer under "Read the post", so the card reads as content first and data second.
  • A meter shows the share against the average of the list, so "0%" and "33.8%" mean something without a legend.
  • The 3px outline with an offset becomes a 2px tinted ring and a soft glow; the weak post still stands out, but no longer looks like a validation error.
  • "Suggest improvements" sits inside the weak card's footer instead of floating above the tag, and keeps the DEVCON liquid on hover.

Advisor bar

client-extension-content-advisor/ldp-content-advisor.js
  • The bar sits above the grid, in the page flow, so nothing covers the first card of the second row any more.
  • It carries the period and the totals the pill showed, plus the legend for the two chips, so a visitor of the demo understands the colors without being told.
  • Turning it off hides the footers and rings, as today.

AI suggestion

client-extension-content-advisor/ldp-content-advisor.js
  • A side panel keeps the blog grid visible, so the presenter can point at the card the suggestion is about.
  • The rewrite is shown as Now against Suggested, which is the comparison the audience makes anyway.
  • The reason becomes two chips; the full text of the agent stays one click away ("Why" expands below), so the panel stays short.

What should we write next?

client-extension-next-posts/ldp-next-posts.js
  • The dashed border and stripes go: they read as a placeholder. The section becomes a white panel like the cards above it.
  • The four-column table becomes a ranking with click-rate bars; clicks stay in the tooltip of each bar.
  • The AI ideas appear in a column beside the ranking, with a dashed magenta frame that says "this came from AI" before the draft is saved.

Live page views

client-extension-live-views/ldp-live-views.js
  • Closed by default as a pill in the bottom right corner: the dot, the count and a sparkline of the last 24 hours.
  • Clicking the pill opens the card above it; the full hourly chart stays for the demo moment when the audience visits.
  • The pill never covers a card's text: it sits in the page's bottom margin and shrinks to the dot at phone width.
