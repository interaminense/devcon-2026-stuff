Swell Lab Use Cases Explanations

DXPLDPAI Hub

1Page views, last 24 hours

  1. 1Client Extensions loads the global script on every page, for signed-in users.
  2. 2Every 30 s, the script asks the Analytics proxy for this page’s views in the last 24 hours. DXP gets them from LDP.

2Custom dashboard page

  1. 1DXP renders the Swell Lab Dashboard page from its template, laid out with fragments.
  2. 2Client Extensions loads the metric card and visitors behavior widgets.
  3. 3Each card lists the LDP channels from the Analytics settings API.
  4. 4Each card asks the Analytics proxy for its metric: site, pages or visit frequency. DXP gets it from LDP.
  5. 5Each card loads and saves its setup in the ldpmetriccards Object.
  6. 6The card asks DXP for a short-lived AI Hub token.
  7. 7The card sends its numbers to the AI Hub agent and shows the insight under the chart.

3Blogs

  1. 1CMS 2.0 blog posts feed the collection display and the post display page.
  2. 2DXP renders /blog with one sl-blog-card per post.
  3. 3The browser sends views, impressions and the surfBlogPostClicked click straight to LDP.

3.1Blogs: most viewed and least viewed

  1. 1Client Extensions loads the Content Advisor script on /blog, for editors.
  2. 2It asks the Analytics proxy for clicks per post and marks the most and the least clicked card.
  3. 3It reads the weakest post from the CMS 2.0 API.
  4. 4It asks DXP for a short-lived AI Hub token.
  5. 5It sends the post and its numbers to the POST_ADVISOR agent, which suggests improvements.
  6. 6On Accept, it saves the suggestion as a draft of the same post (PATCH).
  7. 7It opens that draft in the CMS editor.

3.2Blogs: suggested next posts

  1. 1Client Extensions renders the Next posts widget, only for editors.
  2. 2It reads the existing posts from the CMS 2.0 API.
  3. 3It asks the Analytics proxy for clicks per post.
  4. 4It asks the Analytics CMS API how often each post was shown in lists.
  5. 5It asks DXP for a short-lived AI Hub token.
  6. 6It sends the top posts to the NEXT_POSTS agent, which returns three post ideas.
  7. 7It saves the chosen idea as a new draft post (POST).
